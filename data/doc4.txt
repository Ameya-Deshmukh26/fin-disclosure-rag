FORM 8-K EXCERPT - MERGER ANNOUNCEMENT
Registrant: Meridian Financial Corp
Filing Date: 2026-01-08

Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.
Meridian Financial Corp entered into a definitive merger agreement to acquire Coastal Trust Bank for approximately $340 million in an all-stock transaction. The acquisition is expected to close in Q3 2026, subject to regulatory approval and shareholder votes at both companies.

The combined entity is projected to have approximately $12 billion in total assets. Meridian expects the transaction to be accretive to earnings per share within 18 months of closing, with estimated annual cost synergies of $22 million.
