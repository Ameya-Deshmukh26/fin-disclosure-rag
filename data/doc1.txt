Registrant: Meridian Financial Corp
Filing Date: 2026-03-14

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
On March 14, 2026, Meridian Financial Corp reported Q4 net revenue of $482 million, a 12% increase year-over-year. Operating margin expanded to 23.4% from 21.1% in the prior year period, driven primarily by cost reductions in the servicing division. The company noted elevated credit loss provisions of $38 million due to deterioration in the commercial real estate portfolio.

Management guidance for the following fiscal year projects revenue growth of 8-10%, with continued margin expansion contingent on stabilization of the CRE segment.
