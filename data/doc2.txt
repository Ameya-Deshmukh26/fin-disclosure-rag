FORM 10-Q EXCERPT - RISK FACTORS
Registrant: Meridian Financial Corp
Filing Period: Q1 2026

Item 1A. Risk Factors.
Our commercial real estate loan portfolio, representing approximately 18% of total assets, faces heightened risk due to elevated vacancy rates in office properties across our primary markets. As of the reporting period, non-performing CRE loans increased to 2.1% of the CRE portfolio, up from 1.4% in the prior quarter.

We have increased our allowance for credit losses by $15 million in response to these trends. Management believes current reserves are adequate, but continued deterioration in office occupancy could require additional provisions in future periods.
