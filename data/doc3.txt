FORM 8-K EXCERPT - EXECUTIVE COMPENSATION
Registrant: Meridian Financial Corp
Filing Date: 2026-02-20

Item 5.02 Departure/Election of Directors; Compensation Arrangements.
The Compensation Committee approved a new executive compensation structure effective fiscal year 2026, tying 60% of CEO variable compensation to risk-adjusted return on capital rather than raw net income growth. This change was made in direct response to shareholder feedback regarding compensation alignment with credit risk outcomes following the 2025 CRE portfolio deterioration.

Base salary for the CEO remains unchanged at $1.2 million. Target total compensation is $8.5 million, with a maximum payout of $14 million contingent on performance metrics.
