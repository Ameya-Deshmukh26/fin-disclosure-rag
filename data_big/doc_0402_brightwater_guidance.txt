Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Brightwater Capital Partners revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2592 million and $2632 million, versus prior guidance of $2587 million
to $2642 million. The revision reflects elevated credit costs. Expected full-year
operating margin is 21%. Capital expenditure guidance is $22 million.
