FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Ridgeline Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-07-11

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Ridgeline Trust Company revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2160 million and $2200 million, versus prior guidance of $2155 million
to $2210 million. The revision reflects softer loan demand. Expected full-year
operating margin is 15%. Capital expenditure guidance is $50 million.
