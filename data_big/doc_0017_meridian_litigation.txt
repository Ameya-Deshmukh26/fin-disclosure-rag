FORM 8-K EXCERPT - LEGAL PROCEEDINGS
Registrant: Meridian Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-06-24

Item 8.01 Other Events.
Meridian Regional Bank disclosed a settlement of $4 million related to regulatory
inquiries regarding fee structures. The company admitted no wrongdoing as part of the
resolution. The settlement is expected to be paid within 9 months.
An additional $10 million reserve was established for related matters.
