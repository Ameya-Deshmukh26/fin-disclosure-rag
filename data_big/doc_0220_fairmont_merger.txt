FORM 8-K EXCERPT - MATERIAL DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT
Registrant: Fairmont Capital Partners
Filing Date: 2026-05-17

Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.
Fairmont Capital Partners entered a definitive agreement to acquire Granite State Bank for approximately
$2580 million in an all-stock transaction, expected to close in Q1 2026
subject to regulatory approval. The combined entity would hold approximately
$6.0 billion in total assets. Estimated annual cost synergies are
$32 million, with the transaction accretive to EPS within 12 months.
