FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Ashcroft Holdings
Filing Date: 2026-07-23

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Ashcroft Holdings revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$504 million and $544 million, versus prior guidance of $499 million
to $554 million. The revision reflects integration expenses from recent acquisitions. Expected full-year
operating margin is 25%. Capital expenditure guidance is $24 million.
