Registrant: Oakhaven Regional Bank

Item 8.01 Other Events.
Oakhaven Regional Bank disclosed a settlement of $10 million related to regulatory
inquiries regarding mortgage underwriting standards. The company admitted no wrongdoing as part of the
resolution. The settlement is expected to be paid within 9 months.
An additional $10 million reserve was established for related matters.
