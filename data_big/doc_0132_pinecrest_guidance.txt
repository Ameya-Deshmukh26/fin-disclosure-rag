Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Pinecrest Capital Partners revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$972 million and $1012 million, versus prior guidance of $967 million
to $1022 million. The revision reflects elevated credit costs. Expected full-year
operating margin is 15%. Capital expenditure guidance is $52 million.
