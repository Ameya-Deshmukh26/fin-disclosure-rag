FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Ashcroft Financial Corp
Filing Date: 2026-08-10

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Ashcroft Financial Corp reported quarterly net revenue of $367 million, a 8% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 23%, compared to 24% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $39 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $0.91. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 7% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
