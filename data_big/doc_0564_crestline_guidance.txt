Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Crestline Holdings revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$3564 million and $3604 million, versus prior guidance of $3559 million
to $3614 million. The revision reflects integration expenses from recent acquisitions. Expected full-year
