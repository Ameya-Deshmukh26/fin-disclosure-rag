Registrant: Silverton Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-01-09

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Silverton Regional Bank revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2268 million and $2308 million, versus prior guidance of $2263 million
to $2318 million. The revision reflects stronger fee income. Expected full-year
operating margin is 22%. Capital expenditure guidance is $68 million.
