FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Pinecrest Holdings
Filing Date: 2026-08-26

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Pinecrest Holdings reported quarterly net revenue of $1123 million, a 2% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 23%, compared to 22% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $27 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $2.89. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 7% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
