FORM 8-K EXCERPT - LEGAL PROCEEDINGS
Registrant: Ashcroft Financial Corp
Filing Date: 2026-12-22

Item 8.01 Other Events.
Ashcroft Financial Corp disclosed a settlement of $6 million related to regulatory
inquiries regarding loan servicing practices. The company admitted no wrongdoing as part of the
resolution. The settlement is expected to be paid within 15 months.
An additional $10 million reserve was established for related matters.
