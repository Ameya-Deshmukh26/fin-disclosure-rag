Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Kingsley Holdings revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2844 million and $2884 million, versus prior guidance of $2839 million
to $2894 million. The revision reflects integration expenses from recent acquisitions. Expected full-year
operating margin is 19%. Capital expenditure guidance is $64 million.
