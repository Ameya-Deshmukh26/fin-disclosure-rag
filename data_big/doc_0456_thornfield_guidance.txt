Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Thornfield Financial Corp revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2916 million and $2956 million, versus prior guidance of $2911 million
to $2966 million. The revision reflects improved net interest margin. Expected full-year
operating margin is 20%. Capital expenditure guidance is $26 million.
