FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Harborview Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-01-09

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Harborview Trust Company revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1260 million and $1300 million, versus prior guidance of $1255 million
to $1310 million. The revision reflects softer loan demand. Expected full-year
operating margin is 19%. Capital expenditure guidance is $50 million.
