FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Lakemont Financial Corp
Filing Date: 2026-07-03

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Lakemont Financial Corp revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$3096 million and $3136 million, versus prior guidance of $3091 million
to $3146 million. The revision reflects improved net interest margin. Expected full-year
operating margin is 17%. Capital expenditure guidance is $56 million.
