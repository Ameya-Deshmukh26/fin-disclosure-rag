Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Silverton Holdings revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2304 million and $2344 million, versus prior guidance of $2299 million
to $2354 million. The revision reflects integration expenses from recent acquisitions. Expected full-year
operating margin is 17%. Capital expenditure guidance is $24 million.
