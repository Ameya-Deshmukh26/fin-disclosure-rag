FORM 8-K EXCERPT - EXECUTIVE COMPENSATION
Registrant: Northgate Holdings
Filing Date: 2026-04-26

Item 5.02 Compensation Arrangements.
The Compensation Committee approved a structure tying 65% of CEO variable
compensation to risk-adjusted return on capital. Base salary remains $1.48 million.
Target total compensation is $8 million, with maximum payout of $24 million.
The change responds to shareholder feedback on compensation alignment with credit risk outcomes.
