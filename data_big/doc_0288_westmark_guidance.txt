FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Westmark Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-01-25

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Westmark Regional Bank revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1908 million and $1948 million, versus prior guidance of $1903 million
to $1958 million. The revision reflects stronger fee income. Expected full-year
operating margin is 17%. Capital expenditure guidance is $58 million.
