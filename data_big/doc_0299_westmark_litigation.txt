FORM 8-K EXCERPT - LEGAL PROCEEDINGS
Registrant: Westmark Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-12-02

Item 8.01 Other Events.
Westmark Trust Company disclosed a settlement of $24 million related to regulatory
inquiries regarding overdraft fee assessment. The company admitted no wrongdoing as part of the
resolution. The settlement is expected to be paid within 15 months.
An additional $4 million reserve was established for related matters.
