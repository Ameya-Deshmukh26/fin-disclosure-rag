FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Crestline Financial Corp
Filing Date: 2026-02-28

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Crestline Financial Corp reported quarterly net revenue of $3937 million, a 5% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 17%, compared to 17% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $29 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $0.91. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 4% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
