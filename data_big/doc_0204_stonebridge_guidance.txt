Registrant: Stonebridge Holdings

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Stonebridge Holdings revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1404 million and $1444 million, versus prior guidance of $1399 million
to $1454 million. The revision reflects integration expenses from recent acquisitions. Expected full-year
operating margin is 21%. Capital expenditure guidance is $24 million.
