FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Ridgeline Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-02-24

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Ridgeline Trust Company reported quarterly net revenue of $2425 million, a -2% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 17%, compared to 21% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $13 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $3.55. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 4% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
