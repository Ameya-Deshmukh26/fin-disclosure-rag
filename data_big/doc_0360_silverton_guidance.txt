FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Silverton Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-01-17

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Silverton Trust Company revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2340 million and $2380 million, versus prior guidance of $2335 million
to $2390 million. The revision reflects softer loan demand. Expected full-year
operating margin is 23%. Capital expenditure guidance is $30 million.
