Registrant: Bluewater Holdings
Filing Date: 2026-01-01

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Bluewater Holdings revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$684 million and $724 million, versus prior guidance of $679 million
to $734 million. The revision reflects integration expenses from recent acquisitions. Expected full-year
operating margin is 22%. Capital expenditure guidance is $54 million.
