FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Ridgeline Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-02-16

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Ridgeline Regional Bank reported quarterly net revenue of $2341 million, a 5% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 17%, compared to 20% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $41 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $2.23. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 10% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
