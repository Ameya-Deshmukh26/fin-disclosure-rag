Registrant: Brightwater Regional Bank

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Brightwater Regional Bank revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2628 million and $2668 million, versus prior guidance of $2623 million
to $2678 million. The revision reflects stronger fee income. Expected full-year
operating margin is 16%. Capital expenditure guidance is $28 million.
