FORM 8-K EXCERPT - LEGAL PROCEEDINGS
Registrant: Crestline Financial Corp
Filing Date: 2026-06-12

Item 8.01 Other Events.
Crestline Financial Corp disclosed a settlement of $6 million related to regulatory
inquiries regarding mortgage underwriting standards. The company admitted no wrongdoing as part of the
resolution. The settlement is expected to be paid within 9 months.
An additional $7 million reserve was established for related matters.
