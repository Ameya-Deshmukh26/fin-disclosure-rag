Registrant: Fairmont Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-01-21

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Fairmont Trust Company revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1620 million and $1660 million, versus prior guidance of $1615 million
to $1670 million. The revision reflects softer loan demand. Expected full-year
operating margin is 24%. Capital expenditure guidance is $60 million.
