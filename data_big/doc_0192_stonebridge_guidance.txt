Registrant: Stonebridge Capital Partners

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Stonebridge Capital Partners revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1332 million and $1372 million, versus prior guidance of $1327 million
to $1382 million. The revision reflects elevated credit costs. Expected full-year
operating margin is 20%. Capital expenditure guidance is $62 million.
