FORM 8-K EXCERPT - MATERIAL DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT
Registrant: Kingsley Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-05-01

Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.
Kingsley Trust Company entered a definitive agreement to acquire Coastal Trust Bank for approximately
$5088 million in an all-stock transaction, expected to close in Q1 2026
subject to regulatory approval. The combined entity would hold approximately
$11.6 billion in total assets. Estimated annual cost synergies are
$8 million, with the transaction accretive to EPS within 12 months.
