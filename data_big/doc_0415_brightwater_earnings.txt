FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Brightwater Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-08-14

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Brightwater Trust Company reported quarterly net revenue of $3055 million, a 12% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 23%, compared to 23% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $23 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $3.55. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 4% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
