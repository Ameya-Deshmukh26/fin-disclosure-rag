FORM 8-K EXCERPT - MATERIAL DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT
Registrant: Ironwood Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-11-19

Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.
Ironwood Trust Company entered a definitive agreement to acquire Pinehill Savings for approximately
$6078 million in an all-stock transaction, expected to close in Q3 2026
subject to regulatory approval. The combined entity would hold approximately
$13.0 billion in total assets. Estimated annual cost synergies are
$14 million, with the transaction accretive to EPS within 24 months.
