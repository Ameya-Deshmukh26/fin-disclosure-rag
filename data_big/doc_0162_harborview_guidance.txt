Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Harborview Capital Partners revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1152 million and $1192 million, versus prior guidance of $1147 million
to $1202 million. The revision reflects elevated credit costs. Expected full-year
operating margin is 23%. Capital expenditure guidance is $32 million.
