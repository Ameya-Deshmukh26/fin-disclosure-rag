Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Ironwood Financial Corp revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$3276 million and $3316 million, versus prior guidance of $3271 million
to $3326 million. The revision reflects improved net interest margin. Expected full-year
operating margin is 25%. Capital expenditure guidance is $36 million.
