FORM 8-K EXCERPT - MATERIAL DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT
Registrant: Ironwood Financial Corp
Filing Date: 2026-11-03

Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.
Ironwood Financial Corp entered a definitive agreement to acquire Pinehill Savings for approximately
$5814 million in an all-stock transaction, expected to close in Q3 2026
subject to regulatory approval. The combined entity would hold approximately
$11.6 billion in total assets. Estimated annual cost synergies are
$18 million, with the transaction accretive to EPS within 24 months.
