FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Lakemont Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-07-19

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Lakemont Trust Company revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$3240 million and $3280 million, versus prior guidance of $3235 million
to $3290 million. The revision reflects softer loan demand. Expected full-year
operating margin is 19%. Capital expenditure guidance is $30 million.
