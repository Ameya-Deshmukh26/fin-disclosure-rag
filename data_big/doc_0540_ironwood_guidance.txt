FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Ironwood Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-01-25

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Ironwood Trust Company revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$3420 million and $3460 million, versus prior guidance of $3415 million
to $3470 million. The revision reflects softer loan demand. Expected full-year
operating margin is 16%. Capital expenditure guidance is $60 million.
