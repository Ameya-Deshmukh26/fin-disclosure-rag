FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Westmark Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-01-05

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Westmark Trust Company revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1980 million and $2020 million, versus prior guidance of $1975 million
to $2030 million. The revision reflects softer loan demand. Expected full-year
operating margin is 18%. Capital expenditure guidance is $20 million.
