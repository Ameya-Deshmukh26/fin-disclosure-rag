Registrant: Westmark Holdings
Filing Date: 2026-07-15

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Westmark Holdings revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1944 million and $1984 million, versus prior guidance of $1939 million
to $1994 million. The revision reflects integration expenses from recent acquisitions. Expected full-year
operating margin is 23%. Capital expenditure guidance is $64 million.
