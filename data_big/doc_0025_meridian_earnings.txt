FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Meridian Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-02-20

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Meridian Trust Company reported quarterly net revenue of $325 million, a 2% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 17%, compared to 18% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $33 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $3.55. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 10% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
