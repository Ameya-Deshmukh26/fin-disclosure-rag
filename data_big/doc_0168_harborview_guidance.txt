Registrant: Harborview Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-01-01

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Harborview Regional Bank revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1188 million and $1228 million, versus prior guidance of $1183 million
to $1238 million. The revision reflects stronger fee income. Expected full-year
operating margin is 18%. Capital expenditure guidance is $38 million.
