FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Meridian Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-07-07

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Meridian Trust Company revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$360 million and $400 million, versus prior guidance of $355 million
to $410 million. The revision reflects softer loan demand. Expected full-year
operating margin is 23%. Capital expenditure guidance is $50 million.
