Registrant: Ridgeline Capital Partners

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Ridgeline Capital Partners revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2052 million and $2092 million, versus prior guidance of $2047 million
to $2102 million. The revision reflects elevated credit costs. Expected full-year
operating margin is 19%. Capital expenditure guidance is $32 million.
