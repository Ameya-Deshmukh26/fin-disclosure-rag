FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Thornfield Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-08-18

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Thornfield Regional Bank reported quarterly net revenue of $3391 million, a 3% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 23%, compared to 16% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $31 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $2.23. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 7% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
