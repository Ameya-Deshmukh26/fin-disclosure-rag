Registrant: Pinecrest Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-07-23

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Pinecrest Regional Bank revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1008 million and $1048 million, versus prior guidance of $1003 million
to $1058 million. The revision reflects stronger fee income. Expected full-year
operating margin is 21%. Capital expenditure guidance is $58 million.
