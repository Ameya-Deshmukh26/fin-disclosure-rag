FORM 8-K EXCERPT - MATERIAL DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT
Registrant: Ashcroft Financial Corp
Filing Date: 2026-11-19

Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.
Ashcroft Financial Corp entered a definitive agreement to acquire Pinehill Savings for approximately
$534 million in an all-stock transaction, expected to close in Q3 2026
subject to regulatory approval. The combined entity would hold approximately
$14.4 billion in total assets. Estimated annual cost synergies are
$14 million, with the transaction accretive to EPS within 24 months.
