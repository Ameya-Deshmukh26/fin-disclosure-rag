Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Harborview Financial Corp revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1116 million and $1156 million, versus prior guidance of $1111 million
to $1166 million. The revision reflects improved net interest margin. Expected full-year
operating margin is 17%. Capital expenditure guidance is $26 million.
