FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Silverton Holdings
Filing Date: 2026-02-12

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Silverton Holdings reported quarterly net revenue of $2593 million, a 3% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 17%, compared to 23% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $37 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $2.89. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 10% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
