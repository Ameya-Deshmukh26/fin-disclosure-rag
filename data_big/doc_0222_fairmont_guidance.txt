Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Fairmont Capital Partners revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1512 million and $1552 million, versus prior guidance of $1507 million
to $1562 million. The revision reflects elevated credit costs. Expected full-year
operating margin is 17%. Capital expenditure guidance is $42 million.
