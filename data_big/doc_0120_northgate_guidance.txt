FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Northgate Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-01-25

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Northgate Trust Company revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$900 million and $940 million, versus prior guidance of $895 million
to $950 million. The revision reflects softer loan demand. Expected full-year
operating margin is 25%. Capital expenditure guidance is $40 million.
