FORM 8-K EXCERPT - MATERIAL DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT
Registrant: Harborview Holdings
Filing Date: 2026-05-13

Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.
Harborview Holdings entered a definitive agreement to acquire Granite State Bank for approximately
$2052 million in an all-stock transaction, expected to close in Q1 2026
subject to regulatory approval. The combined entity would hold approximately
$18.6 billion in total assets. Estimated annual cost synergies are
$12 million, with the transaction accretive to EPS within 12 months.
