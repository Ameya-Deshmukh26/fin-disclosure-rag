Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Ridgeline Financial Corp revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2016 million and $2056 million, versus prior guidance of $2011 million
to $2066 million. The revision reflects improved net interest margin. Expected full-year
operating margin is 24%. Capital expenditure guidance is $26 million.
