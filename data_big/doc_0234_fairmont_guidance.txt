Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Fairmont Holdings revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1584 million and $1624 million, versus prior guidance of $1579 million
to $1634 million. The revision reflects integration expenses from recent acquisitions. Expected full-year
operating margin is 18%. Capital expenditure guidance is $54 million.
