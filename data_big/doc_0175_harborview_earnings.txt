FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Harborview Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-08-22

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Harborview Trust Company reported quarterly net revenue of $1375 million, a 0% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 23%, compared to 25% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $23 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $3.55. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 7% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
