FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Northgate Capital Partners
Filing Date: 2026-02-12

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Northgate Capital Partners reported quarterly net revenue of $829 million, a -2% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 17%, compared to 24% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $25 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $1.57. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 10% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
