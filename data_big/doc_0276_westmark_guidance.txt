Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Westmark Financial Corp revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1836 million and $1876 million, versus prior guidance of $1831 million
to $1886 million. The revision reflects improved net interest margin. Expected full-year
operating margin is 16%. Capital expenditure guidance is $46 million.
