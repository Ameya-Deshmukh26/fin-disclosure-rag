FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Stonebridge Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-07-15

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Stonebridge Trust Company revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1440 million and $1480 million, versus prior guidance of $1435 million
to $1490 million. The revision reflects softer loan demand. Expected full-year
operating margin is 16%. Capital expenditure guidance is $30 million.
