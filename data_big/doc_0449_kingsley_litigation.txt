Registrant: Kingsley Trust Company

Item 8.01 Other Events.
Kingsley Trust Company disclosed a settlement of $18 million related to regulatory
inquiries regarding disclosure timing. The company admitted no wrongdoing as part of the
resolution. The settlement is expected to be paid within 9 months.
An additional $10 million reserve was established for related matters.
