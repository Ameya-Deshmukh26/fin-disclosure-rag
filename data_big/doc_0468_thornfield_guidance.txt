Registrant: Thornfield Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-01-05

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Thornfield Regional Bank revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2988 million and $3028 million, versus prior guidance of $2983 million
to $3038 million. The revision reflects stronger fee income. Expected full-year
operating margin is 21%. Capital expenditure guidance is $38 million.
