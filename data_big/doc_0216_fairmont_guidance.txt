Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Fairmont Financial Corp revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1476 million and $1516 million, versus prior guidance of $1471 million
to $1526 million. The revision reflects improved net interest margin. Expected full-year
operating margin is 22%. Capital expenditure guidance is $36 million.
