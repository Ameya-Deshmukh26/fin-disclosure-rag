Registrant: Lakemont Capital Partners

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Lakemont Capital Partners revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$3132 million and $3172 million, versus prior guidance of $3127 million
to $3182 million. The revision reflects elevated credit costs. Expected full-year
operating margin is 23%. Capital expenditure guidance is $62 million.
