Registrant: Northgate Capital Partners

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Northgate Capital Partners revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$792 million and $832 million, versus prior guidance of $787 million
to $842 million. The revision reflects elevated credit costs. Expected full-year
operating margin is 18%. Capital expenditure guidance is $22 million.
