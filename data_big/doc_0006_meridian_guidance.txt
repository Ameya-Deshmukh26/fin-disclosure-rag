FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Meridian Financial Corp
Filing Date: 2026-07-19

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Meridian Financial Corp revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$216 million and $256 million, versus prior guidance of $211 million
to $266 million. The revision reflects improved net interest margin. Expected full-year
operating margin is 21%. Capital expenditure guidance is $26 million.
