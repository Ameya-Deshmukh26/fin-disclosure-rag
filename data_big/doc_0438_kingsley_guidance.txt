FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Kingsley Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-07-27

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Kingsley Regional Bank revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2808 million and $2848 million, versus prior guidance of $2803 million
to $2858 million. The revision reflects stronger fee income. Expected full-year
operating margin is 24%. Capital expenditure guidance is $58 million.
