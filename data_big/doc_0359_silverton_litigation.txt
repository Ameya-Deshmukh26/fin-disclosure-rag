FORM 8-K EXCERPT - LEGAL PROCEEDINGS
Registrant: Silverton Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-12-14

Item 8.01 Other Events.
Silverton Trust Company disclosed a settlement of $8 million related to regulatory
inquiries regarding data privacy compliance. The company admitted no wrongdoing as part of the
resolution. The settlement is expected to be paid within 15 months.
An additional $10 million reserve was established for related matters.
