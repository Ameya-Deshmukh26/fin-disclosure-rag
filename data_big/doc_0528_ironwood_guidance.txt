Registrant: Ironwood Regional Bank

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Ironwood Regional Bank revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$3348 million and $3388 million, versus prior guidance of $3343 million
to $3398 million. The revision reflects stronger fee income. Expected full-year
operating margin is 15%. Capital expenditure guidance is $48 million.
