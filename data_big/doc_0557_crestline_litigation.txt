FORM 8-K EXCERPT - LEGAL PROCEEDINGS
Registrant: Crestline Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-06-20

Item 8.01 Other Events.
Crestline Regional Bank disclosed a settlement of $30 million related to regulatory
inquiries regarding anti-money-laundering controls. The company admitted no wrongdoing as part of the
resolution. The settlement is expected to be paid within 9 months.
An additional $10 million reserve was established for related matters.
