FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Meridian Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-07-27

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Meridian Regional Bank revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$288 million and $328 million, versus prior guidance of $283 million
to $338 million. The revision reflects stronger fee income. Expected full-year
operating margin is 22%. Capital expenditure guidance is $38 million.
