FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Fairmont Capital Partners
Filing Date: 2026-02-08

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Fairmont Capital Partners reported quarterly net revenue of $1669 million, a 4% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 17%, compared to 23% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $25 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $1.57. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 4% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
