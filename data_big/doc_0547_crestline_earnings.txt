FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Crestline Capital Partners
Filing Date: 2026-08-18

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Crestline Capital Partners reported quarterly net revenue of $3979 million, a 11% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 23%, compared to 23% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $35 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $1.57. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 10% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
