FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Ashcroft Financial Corp
Filing Date: 2026-01-25

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Ashcroft Financial Corp revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$396 million and $436 million, versus prior guidance of $391 million
to $446 million. The revision reflects improved net interest margin. Expected full-year
operating margin is 18%. Capital expenditure guidance is $56 million.
