Registrant: Silverton Financial Corp

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Silverton Financial Corp revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2196 million and $2236 million, versus prior guidance of $2191 million
to $2246 million. The revision reflects improved net interest margin. Expected full-year
operating margin is 21%. Capital expenditure guidance is $56 million.
