FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Brightwater Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-01-01

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Brightwater Trust Company revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2700 million and $2740 million, versus prior guidance of $2695 million
to $2750 million. The revision reflects softer loan demand. Expected full-year
operating margin is 17%. Capital expenditure guidance is $40 million.
