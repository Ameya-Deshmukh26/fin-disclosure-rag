Registrant: Calderwood Regional Bank

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Calderwood Regional Bank revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1728 million and $1768 million, versus prior guidance of $1723 million
to $1778 million. The revision reflects stronger fee income. Expected full-year
operating margin is 20%. Capital expenditure guidance is $28 million.
