Registrant: Stonebridge Financial Corp
Filing Date: 2026-07-27

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Stonebridge Financial Corp revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1296 million and $1336 million, versus prior guidance of $1291 million
to $1346 million. The revision reflects improved net interest margin. Expected full-year
operating margin is 25%. Capital expenditure guidance is $56 million.
