Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Summitvale Trust Company revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$3780 million and $3820 million, versus prior guidance of $3775 million
to $3830 million. The revision reflects softer loan demand. Expected full-year
operating margin is 21%. Capital expenditure guidance is $20 million.
