FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Crestline Holdings
Filing Date: 2026-08-26

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Crestline Holdings reported quarterly net revenue of $4063 million, a 4% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 23%, compared to 24% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $47 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $2.89. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 4% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
