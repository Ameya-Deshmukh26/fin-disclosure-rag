FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Northgate Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-01-17

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Northgate Regional Bank revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$828 million and $868 million, versus prior guidance of $823 million
to $878 million. The revision reflects stronger fee income. Expected full-year
operating margin is 24%. Capital expenditure guidance is $28 million.
