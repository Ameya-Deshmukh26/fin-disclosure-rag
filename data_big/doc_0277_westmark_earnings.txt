FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Westmark Capital Partners
Filing Date: 2026-02-20

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Westmark Capital Partners reported quarterly net revenue of $2089 million, a 7% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 17%, compared to 17% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $45 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $1.57. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 10% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
