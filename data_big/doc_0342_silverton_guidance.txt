Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Silverton Capital Partners revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2232 million and $2272 million, versus prior guidance of $2227 million
to $2282 million. The revision reflects elevated credit costs. Expected full-year
operating margin is 16%. Capital expenditure guidance is $62 million.
