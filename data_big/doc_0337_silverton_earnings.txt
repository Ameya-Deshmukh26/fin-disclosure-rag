FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Silverton Capital Partners
Filing Date: 2026-02-04

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Silverton Capital Partners reported quarterly net revenue of $2509 million, a 10% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 17%, compared to 22% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $25 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $1.57. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 7% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
