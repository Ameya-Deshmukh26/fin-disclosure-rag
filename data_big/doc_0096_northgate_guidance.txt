FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Northgate Financial Corp
Filing Date: 2026-01-09

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Northgate Financial Corp revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$756 million and $796 million, versus prior guidance of $751 million
to $806 million. The revision reflects improved net interest margin. Expected full-year
operating margin is 23%. Capital expenditure guidance is $66 million.
