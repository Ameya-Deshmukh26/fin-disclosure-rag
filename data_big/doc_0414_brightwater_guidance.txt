Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Brightwater Holdings revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2664 million and $2704 million, versus prior guidance of $2659 million
to $2714 million. The revision reflects integration expenses from recent acquisitions. Expected full-year
operating margin is 22%. Capital expenditure guidance is $34 million.
