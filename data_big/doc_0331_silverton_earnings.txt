FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Silverton Financial Corp
Filing Date: 2026-08-14

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Silverton Financial Corp reported quarterly net revenue of $2467 million, a 4% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 23%, compared to 16% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $19 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $0.91. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 10% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
