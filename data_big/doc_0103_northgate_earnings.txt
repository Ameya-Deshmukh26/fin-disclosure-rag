FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Northgate Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-08-02

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Northgate Regional Bank reported quarterly net revenue of $871 million, a 4% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 23%, compared to 19% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $31 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $2.23. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 7% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
