Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Thornfield Holdings revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$3024 million and $3064 million, versus prior guidance of $3019 million
to $3074 million. The revision reflects integration expenses from recent acquisitions. Expected full-year
operating margin is 16%. Capital expenditure guidance is $44 million.
