FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Thornfield Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-01-13

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Thornfield Trust Company revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$3060 million and $3100 million, versus prior guidance of $3055 million
to $3110 million. The revision reflects softer loan demand. Expected full-year
operating margin is 22%. Capital expenditure guidance is $50 million.
