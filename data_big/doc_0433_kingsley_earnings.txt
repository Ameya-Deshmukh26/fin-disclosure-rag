FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Kingsley Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-02-12

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Kingsley Regional Bank reported quarterly net revenue of $3181 million, a 11% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 17%, compared to 19% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $41 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $2.23. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 4% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
