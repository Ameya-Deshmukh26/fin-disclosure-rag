Registrant: Fairmont Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-01-13

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Fairmont Regional Bank revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1548 million and $1588 million, versus prior guidance of $1543 million
to $1598 million. The revision reflects stronger fee income. Expected full-year
operating margin is 23%. Capital expenditure guidance is $48 million.
