Registrant: Northgate Holdings
Filing Date: 2026-07-07

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Northgate Holdings revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$864 million and $904 million, versus prior guidance of $859 million
to $914 million. The revision reflects integration expenses from recent acquisitions. Expected full-year
operating margin is 19%. Capital expenditure guidance is $34 million.
