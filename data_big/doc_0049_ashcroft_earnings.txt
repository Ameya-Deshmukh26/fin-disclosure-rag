FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Ashcroft Holdings
Filing Date: 2026-02-08

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Ashcroft Holdings reported quarterly net revenue of $493 million, a 7% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 17%, compared to 20% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $17 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $2.89. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 7% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
