FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Bluewater Financial Corp
Filing Date: 2026-07-03

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Bluewater Financial Corp revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$576 million and $616 million, versus prior guidance of $571 million
to $626 million. The revision reflects improved net interest margin. Expected full-year
operating margin is 15%. Capital expenditure guidance is $36 million.
