FORM 8-K EXCERPT - LEGAL PROCEEDINGS
Registrant: Summitvale Holdings
Filing Date: 2026-06-16

Item 8.01 Other Events.
Summitvale Holdings disclosed a settlement of $34 million related to regulatory
inquiries regarding overdraft fee assessment. The company admitted no wrongdoing as part of the
resolution. The settlement is expected to be paid within 9 months.
An additional $10 million reserve was established for related matters.
