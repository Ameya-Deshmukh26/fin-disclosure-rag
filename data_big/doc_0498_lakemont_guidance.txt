FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Lakemont Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-07-11

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Lakemont Regional Bank revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$3168 million and $3208 million, versus prior guidance of $3163 million
to $3218 million. The revision reflects stronger fee income. Expected full-year
operating margin is 18%. Capital expenditure guidance is $68 million.
