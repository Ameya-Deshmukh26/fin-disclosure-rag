FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Silverton Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-08-02

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Silverton Trust Company reported quarterly net revenue of $2635 million, a 9% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 23%, compared to 18% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $43 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $3.55. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 7% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
