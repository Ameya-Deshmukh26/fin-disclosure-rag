FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Stonebridge Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-07-07

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Stonebridge Regional Bank revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1368 million and $1408 million, versus prior guidance of $1363 million
to $1418 million. The revision reflects stronger fee income. Expected full-year
operating margin is 15%. Capital expenditure guidance is $68 million.
