FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Bluewater Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-07-19

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Bluewater Trust Company revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$720 million and $760 million, versus prior guidance of $715 million
to $770 million. The revision reflects softer loan demand. Expected full-year
operating margin is 17%. Capital expenditure guidance is $60 million.
