FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Summitvale Capital Partners
Filing Date: 2026-02-24

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Summitvale Capital Partners reported quarterly net revenue of $4189 million, a 3% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 17%, compared to 20% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $25 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $1.57. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 4% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
