FORM 10-Q EXCERPT - RISK FACTORS
Registrant: Lakemont Financial Corp
Filing Period: Q3 2026

Item 1A. Risk Factors.
Our commercial real estate loan portfolio represents approximately 11% of total assets.
Non-performing CRE loans stand at 2.05% of the CRE portfolio, compared to 1.92%
in the prior quarter. We increased our allowance for credit losses by $33 million.
Concentration risk in Southeast markets represents a material exposure. Management believes
current reserves are adequate but continued deterioration could require further provisions.
