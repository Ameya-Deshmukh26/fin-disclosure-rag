FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Harborview Holdings
Filing Date: 2026-02-04

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Harborview Holdings reported quarterly net revenue of $1333 million, a 13% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 17%, compared to 19% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $17 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $2.89. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 10% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
