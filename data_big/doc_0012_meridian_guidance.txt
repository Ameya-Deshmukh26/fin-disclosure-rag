Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Meridian Capital Partners revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$252 million and $292 million, versus prior guidance of $247 million
to $302 million. The revision reflects elevated credit costs. Expected full-year
operating margin is 16%. Capital expenditure guidance is $32 million.
