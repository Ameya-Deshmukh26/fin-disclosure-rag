FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Calderwood Capital Partners
Filing Date: 2026-08-14

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Calderwood Capital Partners reported quarterly net revenue of $1879 million, a -4% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 23%, compared to 20% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $15 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $1.57. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 7% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
