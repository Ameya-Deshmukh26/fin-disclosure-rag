FORM 8-K EXCERPT - LEGAL PROCEEDINGS
Registrant: Summitvale Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-12-06

Item 8.01 Other Events.
Summitvale Trust Company disclosed a settlement of $12 million related to regulatory
inquiries regarding anti-money-laundering controls. The company admitted no wrongdoing as part of the
resolution. The settlement is expected to be paid within 15 months.
An additional $7 million reserve was established for related matters.
