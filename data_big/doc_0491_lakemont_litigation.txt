FORM 8-K EXCERPT - LEGAL PROCEEDINGS
Registrant: Lakemont Capital Partners
Filing Date: 2026-12-18

Item 8.01 Other Events.
Lakemont Capital Partners disclosed a settlement of $34 million related to regulatory
inquiries regarding disclosure timing. The company admitted no wrongdoing as part of the
resolution. The settlement is expected to be paid within 15 months.
An additional $7 million reserve was established for related matters.
