Registrant: Ashcroft Capital Partners

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Ashcroft Capital Partners revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$432 million and $472 million, versus prior guidance of $427 million
to $482 million. The revision reflects elevated credit costs. Expected full-year
operating margin is 24%. Capital expenditure guidance is $62 million.
