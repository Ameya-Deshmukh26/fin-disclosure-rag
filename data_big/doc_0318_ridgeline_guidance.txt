FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Ridgeline Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-07-03

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Ridgeline Regional Bank revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2088 million and $2128 million, versus prior guidance of $2083 million
to $2138 million. The revision reflects stronger fee income. Expected full-year
operating margin is 25%. Capital expenditure guidance is $38 million.
