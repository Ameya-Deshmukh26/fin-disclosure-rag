FORM 8-K EXCERPT - EXECUTIVE COMPENSATION
Registrant: Ashcroft Holdings
Filing Date: 2026-04-14

Item 5.02 Compensation Arrangements.
The Compensation Committee approved a structure tying 45% of CEO variable
compensation to risk-adjusted return on capital. Base salary remains $1.12 million.
Target total compensation is $11 million, with maximum payout of $20 million.
The change responds to shareholder feedback on compensation alignment with credit risk outcomes.
