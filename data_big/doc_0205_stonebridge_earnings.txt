FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Stonebridge Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-02-28

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Stonebridge Trust Company reported quarterly net revenue of $1585 million, a 11% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 17%, compared to 22% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $13 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $3.55. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 10% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
