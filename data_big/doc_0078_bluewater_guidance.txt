FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Bluewater Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-07-11

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Bluewater Regional Bank revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$648 million and $688 million, versus prior guidance of $643 million
to $698 million. The revision reflects stronger fee income. Expected full-year
operating margin is 16%. Capital expenditure guidance is $48 million.
