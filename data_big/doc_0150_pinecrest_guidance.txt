FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Pinecrest Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-07-03

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Pinecrest Trust Company revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1080 million and $1120 million, versus prior guidance of $1075 million
to $1130 million. The revision reflects softer loan demand. Expected full-year
operating margin is 22%. Capital expenditure guidance is $20 million.
