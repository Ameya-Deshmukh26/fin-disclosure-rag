FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Meridian Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-02-12

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Meridian Regional Bank reported quarterly net revenue of $241 million, a 9% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 17%, compared to 17% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $21 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $2.23. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 7% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
