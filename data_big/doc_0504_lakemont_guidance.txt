Registrant: Lakemont Holdings

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Lakemont Holdings revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$3204 million and $3244 million, versus prior guidance of $3199 million
to $3254 million. The revision reflects integration expenses from recent acquisitions. Expected full-year
operating margin is 24%. Capital expenditure guidance is $24 million.
