FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Meridian Holdings
Filing Date: 2026-01-17

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Meridian Holdings revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$324 million and $364 million, versus prior guidance of $319 million
to $374 million. The revision reflects integration expenses from recent acquisitions. Expected full-year
operating margin is 17%. Capital expenditure guidance is $44 million.
