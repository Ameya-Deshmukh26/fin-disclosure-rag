Registrant: Crestline Regional Bank

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Crestline Regional Bank revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$3528 million and $3568 million, versus prior guidance of $3523 million
to $3578 million. The revision reflects stronger fee income. Expected full-year
operating margin is 23%. Capital expenditure guidance is $28 million.
