FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Ashcroft Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-01-13

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Ashcroft Trust Company revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$540 million and $580 million, versus prior guidance of $535 million
to $590 million. The revision reflects softer loan demand. Expected full-year
operating margin is 20%. Capital expenditure guidance is $30 million.
