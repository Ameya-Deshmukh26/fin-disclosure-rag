FORM 8-K EXCERPT - MATERIAL DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT
Registrant: Kingsley Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-05-21

Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.
Kingsley Regional Bank entered a definitive agreement to acquire Granite State Bank for approximately
$4956 million in an all-stock transaction, expected to close in Q1 2026
subject to regulatory approval. The combined entity would hold approximately
$18.6 billion in total assets. Estimated annual cost synergies are
$24 million, with the transaction accretive to EPS within 12 months.
