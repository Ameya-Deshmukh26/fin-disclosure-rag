FORM 8-K EXCERPT - LEGAL PROCEEDINGS
Registrant: Lakemont Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-06-08

Item 8.01 Other Events.
Lakemont Regional Bank disclosed a settlement of $12 million related to regulatory
inquiries regarding loan servicing practices. The company admitted no wrongdoing as part of the
resolution. The settlement is expected to be paid within 9 months.
An additional $4 million reserve was established for related matters.
