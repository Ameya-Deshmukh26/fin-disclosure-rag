Registrant: Harborview Holdings

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Harborview Holdings revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1224 million and $1264 million, versus prior guidance of $1219 million
to $1274 million. The revision reflects integration expenses from recent acquisitions. Expected full-year
operating margin is 24%. Capital expenditure guidance is $44 million.
