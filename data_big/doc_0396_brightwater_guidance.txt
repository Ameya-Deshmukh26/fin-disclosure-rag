FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Brightwater Financial Corp
Filing Date: 2026-01-13

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Brightwater Financial Corp revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2556 million and $2596 million, versus prior guidance of $2551 million
to $2606 million. The revision reflects improved net interest margin. Expected full-year
operating margin is 15%. Capital expenditure guidance is $66 million.
