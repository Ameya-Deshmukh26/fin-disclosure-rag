FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Ashcroft Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-01-05

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Ashcroft Regional Bank revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$468 million and $508 million, versus prior guidance of $463 million
to $518 million. The revision reflects stronger fee income. Expected full-year
operating margin is 19%. Capital expenditure guidance is $68 million.
