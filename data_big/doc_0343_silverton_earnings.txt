FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Silverton Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-08-22

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Silverton Regional Bank reported quarterly net revenue of $2551 million, a -3% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 23%, compared to 17% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $31 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $2.23. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 4% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
