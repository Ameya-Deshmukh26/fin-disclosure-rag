FORM 8-K EXCERPT - FORWARD GUIDANCE REVISION
Registrant: Calderwood Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-07-27

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Calderwood Trust Company revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$1800 million and $1840 million, versus prior guidance of $1795 million
to $1850 million. The revision reflects softer loan demand. Expected full-year
operating margin is 21%. Capital expenditure guidance is $40 million.
