FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Lakemont Financial Corp
Filing Date: 2026-02-16

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Lakemont Financial Corp reported quarterly net revenue of $3517 million, a 2% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 17%, compared to 23% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $9 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $0.91. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 7% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
