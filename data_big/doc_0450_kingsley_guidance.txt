Registrant: Kingsley Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-07-07

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Kingsley Trust Company revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2880 million and $2920 million, versus prior guidance of $2875 million
to $2930 million. The revision reflects softer loan demand. Expected full-year
operating margin is 25%. Capital expenditure guidance is $20 million.
