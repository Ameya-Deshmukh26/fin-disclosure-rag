Registrant: Bluewater Capital Partners
Filing Date: 2026-01-21

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Bluewater Capital Partners revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$612 million and $652 million, versus prior guidance of $607 million
to $662 million. The revision reflects elevated credit costs. Expected full-year
operating margin is 21%. Capital expenditure guidance is $42 million.
