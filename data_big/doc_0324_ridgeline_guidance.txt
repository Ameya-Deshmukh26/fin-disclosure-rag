Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Ridgeline Holdings revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2124 million and $2164 million, versus prior guidance of $2119 million
to $2174 million. The revision reflects integration expenses from recent acquisitions. Expected full-year
operating margin is 20%. Capital expenditure guidance is $44 million.
