Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Crestline Trust Company revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$3600 million and $3640 million, versus prior guidance of $3595 million
to $3650 million. The revision reflects softer loan demand. Expected full-year
operating margin is 24%. Capital expenditure guidance is $40 million.
