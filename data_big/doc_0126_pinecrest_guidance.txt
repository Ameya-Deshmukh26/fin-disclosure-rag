Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Pinecrest Financial Corp revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$936 million and $976 million, versus prior guidance of $931 million
to $986 million. The revision reflects improved net interest margin. Expected full-year
operating margin is 20%. Capital expenditure guidance is $46 million.
