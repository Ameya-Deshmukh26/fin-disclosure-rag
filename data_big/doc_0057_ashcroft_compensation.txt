FORM 8-K EXCERPT - EXECUTIVE COMPENSATION
Registrant: Ashcroft Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-10-04

Item 5.02 Compensation Arrangements.
The Compensation Committee approved a structure tying 40% of CEO variable
compensation to risk-adjusted return on capital. Base salary remains $0.94 million.
Target total compensation is $8 million, with maximum payout of $12 million.
The change responds to shareholder feedback on compensation alignment with credit risk outcomes.
