FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Westmark Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-08-10

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Westmark Regional Bank reported quarterly net revenue of $2131 million, a 13% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 23%, compared to 23% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $11 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $2.23. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 7% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
