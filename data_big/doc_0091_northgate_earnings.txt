FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Northgate Financial Corp
Filing Date: 2026-08-22

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Northgate Financial Corp reported quarterly net revenue of $787 million, a 11% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 23%, compared to 18% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $19 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $0.91. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 4% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
