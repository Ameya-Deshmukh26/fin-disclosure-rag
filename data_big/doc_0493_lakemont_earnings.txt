FORM 8-K EXCERPT - RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Registrant: Lakemont Regional Bank
Filing Date: 2026-02-24

Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Lakemont Regional Bank reported quarterly net revenue of $3601 million, a 14% change year-over-year.
Operating margin was 17%, compared to 24% in the prior year period.
The company recorded credit loss provisions of $21 million this quarter.
Diluted earnings per share were $2.23. Management guidance projects revenue growth
of 10% for the following fiscal year, contingent on macroeconomic conditions.
