FORM 8-K EXCERPT - MATERIAL DEFINITIVE AGREEMENT
Registrant: Brightwater Trust Company
Filing Date: 2026-11-23

Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.
Brightwater Trust Company entered a definitive agreement to acquire Pinehill Savings for approximately
$4758 million in an all-stock transaction, expected to close in Q3 2026
subject to regulatory approval. The combined entity would hold approximately
$6.0 billion in total assets. Estimated annual cost synergies are
$34 million, with the transaction accretive to EPS within 24 months.
