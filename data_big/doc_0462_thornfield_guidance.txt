Item 2.02 Results of Operations and Financial Condition.
Thornfield Capital Partners revised full-year guidance, now projecting net revenue between
$2952 million and $2992 million, versus prior guidance of $2947 million
to $3002 million. The revision reflects elevated credit costs. Expected full-year
operating margin is 15%. Capital expenditure guidance is $32 million.
